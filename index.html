About Us

A place where learning starts with tradition and ends with technology

Jha Institute of Computer and Technology is a coaching institute dedicated to making computer education simple, practical and accessible. Every day at the institute begins with a prayer to Maa Saraswati, the goddess of knowledge — a reminder of the respect we hold for learning.

Founded by Santosh Jha, whose vision is to bring quality computer education to every student in Sodepur and nearby areas.

Our classes combine whiteboard teaching with live practice on computers, so students do not just memorise concepts — they use them. From computer fundamentals to programming and databases, we guide every student at their own pace.

  • Concept teaching on whiteboard and smart display
  • Practical sessions in our own computer lab
  • Friendly, personal guidance for every student
